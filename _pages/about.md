Hi! I'm Yanghao Chen, a final-year undergraduate at **[Tongji University](https://www.tongji.edu.cn/)**, studying Engineering Mechanics in the **[School of Aerospace Engineering and Applied Mechanics](https://aero-mech.tongji.edu.cn/31828/list.htm)**. My interests center on <strong class="research-keyword">scientific machine learning</strong> and computational mechanics.

I began by fine-tuning <strong class="research-keyword">large language models</strong> with LoRA for question answering in mechanics of materials. I then used <strong class="research-keyword">ConvLSTM</strong> models to forecast concentration and stress images generated by MATLAB–COMSOL simulations of battery materials. My work with <strong class="research-keyword">physics-informed neural networks (PINNs)</strong> compared standard and artificial-viscosity formulations for a one-dimensional Burgers shock problem.

Together, these projects have drawn me toward combining machine learning, physics-based modeling, and <strong class="research-keyword">GPU-accelerated numerical simulation</strong>. My current work focuses on <strong class="research-keyword">neural operators</strong>, which learn mappings between function spaces, and <strong class="research-keyword">hybrid FEM–neural operator solvers</strong> that couple learned local models with finite element methods. I'm particularly interested in making this coupling reliable and efficient, with the longer-term goal of tackling larger and more complex mechanics problems.

At Tongji University, I've worked under the guidance of **[Prof. Ying Zhao](https://aero-mech.tongji.edu.cn/3a/6b/c26961a211563/page.htm)** and **[Prof. Xianyang (Tom) Chen](https://aero-mech.tongji.edu.cn/50/cc/c22274a348364/page.htm)**. I'm currently a visiting undergraduate research intern in the [Department of Civil and Systems Engineering](https://engineering.jhu.edu/case/) at **[Johns Hopkins University](https://www.jhu.edu/)**, working with **[Prof. Somdatta Goswami](https://engineering.jhu.edu/case/faculty/somdatta-goswami/)** in her [Centrum IntelliPhysics](https://sites.google.com/view/centrum-intelliphysics/home) group.

Current Research
======

My current research focuses on GPU-accelerated computational mechanics and scientific machine learning. I am developing hybrid FEM–neural operator solvers in which a local neural operator learns the mapping from interface displacement fields to subdomain stress fields. Interface reaction forces are assembled from the resulting stress fields and returned to the outer finite element solver through iterative coupling.
